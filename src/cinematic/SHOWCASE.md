# Showcase Subject — International Business

International Business is the **gold-standard / reference implementation** for the Presentation Engine.

## Rule

Future engine changes must:

1. Prove they improve International Business first.
2. Preserve IB teaching quality, pacing, and accessibility.
3. Only then roll out platform-wide.

Future subjects must ask:

> Does this subject reach the International Business quality bar?

If not, it isn’t finished.
