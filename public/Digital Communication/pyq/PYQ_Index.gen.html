Method and its limits

Page numbers are approximate. These merged PDFs use compressed object streams, and the text was recovered by inflating every content stream and reading the Tj and TJ operators; the stream index is scaled to the declared page count to estimate a printed page. Use the index to locate the paper within the merged file, then confirm visually. Subject codes, titles, question numbers and module headings are taken verbatim from the extracted text, never inferred.

Caution on scheme drift

These papers were set under earlier schemes. Where a paper for this subject exists at all, its syllabus is close to but not identical with the 2025-scheme syllabus taught by this deck set, and module boundaries in particular have moved. Treat them as a guide to question style, difficulty and mark allocation rather than as a module-by-module question bank.
